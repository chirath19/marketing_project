{% comment %} The line mark in the corner of a card, picked by position. There are eight, because the grids these sit in run to six and the catalogue runs to twenty-eight: with three marks every grid repeated itself twice before it finished, which is the single clearest tell that a page was filled rather than designed. Eight covers a grid outright and makes the catalogue's repeat long enough not to read as one. All eight are drawn on the same 80×64 canvas with the same stroke weights, so they sit as a set rather than as eight borrowed icons, and each says something about the discipline it tends to land on — a lens, a trend, a target, a message, a measure, a schedule, a pair of people, a signal. Decorative: every card states its own subject in words, so there is nothing here for a screen reader to announce. {% endcomment %}
