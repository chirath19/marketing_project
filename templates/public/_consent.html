{% comment %} The consent banner. Decides nothing server-side — it is shown or hidden by Alpine reading `hgConsent.decided()`, because the decision lives in the visitor's own browser (localStorage), not in a session Django can see on first render. Three buttons: accept everything, reject everything, or a settings view with the categories broken out, since "necessary, analytics, advertising" independently is what section 33 asks for. This renders unconditionally — on every page, consented or not — so a visitor who rejected tracking is never shown a banner that looks like it is asking again every time they navigate, and `x-show` is what actually hides it once a decision exists. {% endcomment %}
