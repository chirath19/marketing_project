{% comment %} The ambient field behind a dark section. Four pieces, each doing one job: coloured light that drifts, a measured grid that fades out, a slow conic sweep that keeps the surface from looking like a static gradient, and a film of grain that stops the blurred colour banding on an 8-bit display. All of it is CSS. There is no canvas, no library and no scroll listener, so it costs one composited layer and nothing per frame on the main thread — and the reduced-motion rule in the base layer stops every animation at once for anyone who has asked for that. Purely decorative, and hidden from assistive technology: nothing here carries meaning that the words beside it do not already carry. {% endcomment %}
