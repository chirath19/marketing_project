The card is light by default and dark when the service is featured, so a grid of twenty-eight reads as a page with a few things picked out rather than a wall. Nothing here states a colour: every value comes from `.site-catalog-card` and its `.site-cell-featured` variant, which is the only way two treatments of the same card stay in step. Every card opens with a band of the same height. Five services have a photograph of the actual working surface — a crawl report, a campaign dashboard, a publishing board — and show it; the rest show their discipline's line motif drawn large on a tinted panel. Equal height is the point: a grid where only some cards carry a picture leaves the others with a hole above the price, and a reader reads the hole as a mistake. The motif comes from the category, not from where the card happens to land in the grid. Position chose it once, and put an envelope on "On-page SEO". {% endcomment %}
